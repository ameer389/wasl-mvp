# Phase 06 evidence and rule coverage

Days are patient-relative. D is the task deadline. Evidence must be available by the evaluation day. The most recent eligible occurrence takes precedence, with persisted recording order breaking ties. Confidence at or above 0.70 is required for a definite outcome. Source and event eligibility also apply. The tests in `gapEvidence.test.ts` cover the source boundary, rewind, contrary reports and administrative filtering. The original `gapEngine.test.ts` covers the seeded rules and near misses. `medicationEvidence.test.ts`, `voiceAnswers.test.ts`, and `questionPlanner.test.ts` cover reconciliation, parser claims and scheduling implications.

| Task or report | Source and claim | Confidence and boundary | Expected task assessment and gap | Severity | Supporting evidence and planner behavior |
|---|---|---|---|---:|---|
| Start | Patient, family, explicitly confirmed clinician, or EHR `USAGE`; `NOT_DONE` | ≥0.70, from D+1 | Reported not started, `NOT_STARTED` | 8 | Prior contrary eligible report is retained. Linked clinician route pauses the question. A later task-linked `DONE` independently clears this condition. |
| Stop | Same medication sources; `STILL_TAKING` or matching open `TAKING_UNLISTED` | ≥0.70, immediately | Use of a stopped medicine reported, `STOPPED_STILL_TAKEN` | 9 | Prior contrary report is context. Task-linked `STOPPED` can verify, and an eligible medication-level correction updates the assessment. |
| Dose | Same medication sources; `OLD_DOSE` | ≥0.70, immediately | Previous dose reported, `WRONG_DOSE` | 8 | Distinct from a denial with an unspecified actual dose. Linked clinician review pauses the question. |
| Continuation, wound and dose | Eligible direct report; EHR `USAGE` also eligible for medication tasks; `NOT_DONE` | ≥0.70, from D for continuation/wound; immediate for dose | Reported incomplete, `TASK_NOT_COMPLETED` | 6 | Includes relevant contrary history. Linked clinician review pauses repeat questions. Low-risk cases remain eligible for in-app follow-up. Continuation ignores occurrences after the last treatment day. |
| Open medication, possible overlap | Eligible medication report, `TAKING_UNLISTED`, with an active plan ingredient/class match | ≥0.70, immediately | `DUPLICATE_THERAPY` | 9 | Lists applicable overlaps. High risk if the reported medicine or any matching planned medicine is high-alert. Pending overlap review leaves concurrent use unconfirmed. |
| Open medication, unmatched | Eligible medication report, `TAKING_UNLISTED` | ≥0.70, immediately | `UNLISTED_MED` | 5 | Known identity uses its drug key. Independently unidentified reports keep distinct IDs. Explicit correction updates the effective report. |
| Laboratory | `lab_system`, `LAB_RESULT` or compatible legacy task record | No eligible `DONE` at ≥0.70, from D+1 | `LAB_MISSED`, displayed as lab result unavailable | 6 | Weak records, an eligible system negative and patient/family reports supply context. Missing result keeps patient follow-up eligible. |
| Booking | `appointment_system`, `APPOINTMENT_BOOKING` or compatible legacy task record | No eligible `DONE` at ≥0.70, from D+1 | `FOLLOWUP_NOT_BOOKED`, displayed as booking unconfirmed | 5 | Positive and negative patient reports add context. A later eligible booking resolves the condition. |
| Attendance, missed | `appointment_system`, `APPOINTMENT_ATTENDANCE` or compatible legacy record from the visit day | `NOT_DONE` at ≥0.70, from D+1 | `FOLLOWUP_MISSED` | 6 | Trigger is the eligible negative; patient or family statements supply context. Linked clinician review can pause repeat questions. |
| Attendance, unknown | Matching feed is missing, weak or ineligible | No eligible outcome at ≥0.70, from D+1 | `FOLLOWUP_ATTENDANCE_UNCONFIRMED` | 5 | Patient or family statements remain supporting evidence. The patient question stays eligible after deadline. A matching eligible outcome resolves or reclassifies the condition. |
| Missing patient-side task answer | No eligible direct or medication observation; outside feed tasks | D+2 | `NO_RESPONSE` | 4 | Linked nonqualifying records such as dispensing may appear for context. Unresolved eligible tasks can be asked. |
| Uncertainty | Eligible direct or open report, weak expected feed, or a newer conflicting direct report against a system result | Below 0.70, ambiguous, or conflicting. Immediate when the uncertainty is a separate issue | `LOW_CONFIDENCE`, displayed as evidence needs clarification | 3 | Effective and uncertain records remain distinct. Related open medication mentions group by `reportId`. A matched clinician review pauses the question. A post-deadline missing feed usually uses its specific missing-outcome rule. |

Routing is `CLINICIAN_NOW` for high risk, `CLINICIAN_24H` for medium risk and low-risk uncertainty, and `PATIENT_REMINDER` for other low-risk items. The latter is in-app follow-up. The queue sorts by route before the original numeric score. Rule conditions are independent of administrative filtering and retain their triggering records. Same-day reopening and persistent detection events are assigned to Phase 07.
