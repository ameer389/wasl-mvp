## 1. Gap detection (synthetic scenarios)

**Method.** Seeded generator (seed 20261114): 50 synthetic discharge cases with known, deliberately injected gaps (5 per gap type, some cases with a second gap) and 10 clean controls. The real engine is run on Day 4 for injected cases and on every day 0–14 for controls, and compared with the ground truth.

**Important.** The rules are deterministic and the scenarios were written by the same team, so this proves coverage and correctness of the logic, not real-world accuracy.

| Gap type | Injected | Detected | Recall |
|---|---|---|---|
| New medicine not started (`NOT_STARTED`) | 5 | 5 | 100% |
| Stopped medicine still being taken (`STOPPED_STILL_TAKEN`) | 5 | 5 | 100% |
| Previous dose still in use (`WRONG_DOSE`) | 5 | 5 | 100% |
| Possible duplicate therapy (`DUPLICATE_THERAPY`) | 5 | 5 | 100% |
| Medicine not on the discharge list (`UNLISTED_MED`) | 13 | 13 | 100% |
| Lab test not done (`LAB_MISSED`) | 11 | 11 | 100% |
| Follow-up not booked (`FOLLOWUP_NOT_BOOKED`) | 8 | 8 | 100% |
| Follow-up visit missed (`FOLLOWUP_MISSED`) | 5 | 5 | 100% |
| No response (`NO_RESPONSE`) | 5 | 5 | 100% |
| Low-confidence answer: needs a human (`LOW_CONFIDENCE`) | 5 | 5 | 100% |
| **All** | **67** | **67** | **100%** |

- Clean controls: 10 cases checked on 150 patient-days; 0 cases raised a gap.
- Missed gaps: none.
- Unexpected gaps: none.

**Limitations.** Injected gaps are the textbook form of each problem. Real cases are messier: partial information, conflicting reports, plans with errors. A score of 100% here says the rules do what we designed them to do — nothing more.

## 2. Understanding Saudi-dialect answers (parser)

**Method.** 130 hand-written answers (52 dev / 78 test). The parser was tuned on the dev split only; the test split was evaluated once after freezing it. Details: `validation/README.md`.

| Intent | Precision | Recall | F1 | Test items |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| MENTIONS_MEDS | 1 | 1 | 1 | 8 |
| NEW_DOSE | 1 | 0.75 | 0.86 | 4 |
| NO | 1 | 0.9 | 0.95 | 10 |
| NONE | 1 | 1 | 1 | 5 |
| OLD_DOSE | 1 | 1 | 1 | 5 |
| STILL_TAKING | 1 | 1 | 1 | 6 |
| STOPPED | 1 | 1 | 1 | 6 |
| UNKNOWN | 0.8 | 1 | 0.89 | 8 |
| UNSURE | 1 | 1 | 1 | 14 |
| YES | 1 | 1 | 1 | 12 |

- Test accuracy 97.4% (76/78); hard items 90% (n=20); dev accuracy 100%.
- Wrong answers: 2. With confidence < 0.7 (never trusted, sent to a human): 2. Unsafe (wrong and trusted): 0.
- Medicine-name extraction: 55.6% exact match (n=9).
- Wrong: `u018` «والله ما شفت دوا جديد» — expected NO, got UNKNOWN (confidence 0.2).
- Wrong: `u087` «صرت اخذ ١٤ وحده بالليل» — expected NEW_DOSE, got UNKNOWN (confidence 0.2).

**Limitations.** The same team wrote the answers and the parser, so real accuracy will be lower. Answers are typed, not recorded speech. 130 items is a small set.

## 3. Question reduction

**Method.** For every scenario and every day 0–14: the adaptive plan (only uncertain items, at most 5, plus one open question) versus a full script that asks about every patient-side task due so far, every day.

| | Questions | Per patient-day |
|---|---|---|
| Adaptive (WASL) | 1683 | 1.9 |
| Full script, every day | 8304 | 9.2 |
| Reduction | 79.7% | |

**Assumption.** Minutes saved assume 1.5 minutes per question and a full-script call EVERY day. Daily full-script calls are not current practice, so this is an upper bound, not a measured saving.

## What we have NOT tested yet

- Real patients and families using the app at home
- Real clinicians working the queue, and whether it changes what they do
- Real voice recordings at scale (accents, noise, elderly speech) through speech-to-text
- Integration with a real EHR, lab system or appointment system
- Clinical outcomes: readmissions, adverse drug events, adherence
