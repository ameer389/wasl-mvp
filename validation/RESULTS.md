## 1. Gap detection (synthetic scenarios)

**Method.** Seeded generator (seed 20261114): 50 synthetic discharge cases (5 injections per gap type, some with a second gap) and 10 clean controls. Medication reports below the confidence floor are expected to trigger review instead of an assertion of use. The engine is run on Day 4 for injected cases and on days 0–14 for controls.

**Important.** The rules are deterministic and the scenarios were written by the same team, so this proves coverage and correctness of the logic, not real-world accuracy.

| Gap type | Injected | Detected | Recall |
|---|---|---|---|
| New medicine reported not started (`NOT_STARTED`) | 5 | 5 | 100% |
| Stopped medicine reported in use (`STOPPED_STILL_TAKEN`) | 5 | 5 | 100% |
| Previous dose reported in use (`WRONG_DOSE`) | 5 | 5 | 100% |
| Possible duplicate therapy (`DUPLICATE_THERAPY`) | 3 | 3 | 100% |
| Medicine identity or plan match needs review (`UNLISTED_MED`) | 6 | 6 | 100% |
| Lab result unavailable (`LAB_MISSED`) | 11 | 11 | 100% |
| Follow-up booking unconfirmed (`FOLLOWUP_NOT_BOOKED`) | 8 | 8 | 100% |
| Follow-up reported missed (`FOLLOWUP_MISSED`) | 5 | 5 | 100% |
| No response (`NO_RESPONSE`) | 5 | 5 | 100% |
| Evidence needs clarification (`LOW_CONFIDENCE`) | 14 | 14 | 100% |
| **All** | **67** | **67** | **100%** |

- Clean controls: 10 cases checked on 150 patient-days; 0 cases raised a gap.
- Missed gaps: none.
- Unexpected gaps: none.

**Phase 06 focused cases.** 7 injected cases and 1 clean control evaluated separately. 7 expected gaps detected, 0 misses, 0 unexpected, 1/1 high-risk routes.

| Gap type | Expected | Detected |
|---|---:|---:|
| `LAB_MISSED` | 1 | 1 |
| `FOLLOWUP_MISSED` | 1 | 1 |
| `TASK_NOT_COMPLETED` | 3 | 3 |
| `FOLLOWUP_ATTENDANCE_UNCONFIRMED` | 2 | 2 |

**Limitations.** Injected gaps are the textbook form of each problem. Real cases are messier: partial information, conflicting reports, plans with errors. A score of 100% here says the rules do what we designed them to do — nothing more.

## 3. Question reduction

**Method.** For every scenario and every day 0–14: planned questions with no patient-session history (up to 5 task questions plus the open question when eligible) versus the full daily script. A separate replay assumes each question in one session per day is presented and adds no answers.

| | Questions planned | Per patient-day |
|---|---|---|
| Adaptive (WASL) | 1747 | 1.9 |
| Full script, every day | 8304 | 9.2 |
| Reduction | 79% | |

**Scheduling replay.** 1747 assumed presentations, including 900 open questions. The replay reached 466 of 466 ever-eligible tasks, including 135 of 135 high-risk tasks. The session cap deferred 0 task selections across days. There were 381 task presentations after an earlier presentation of the same task. The replay adds zero answers and runs one daily session.

**Assumption.** The full daily script is a hypothetical baseline. Both counts describe plans. The separate replay assumes presentation and adds zero answers. Staff time and actual user behavior were unmeasured.
